# Third-party assets and attribution

This project incorporates third-party 3D assets. Entries marked **VERIFICAR** are
unresolved: the source file arrived without licence metadata and the terms below are
inference, not confirmation. Resolve those before any public release or distribution.

---

## Patient body — `Assets/Models/Patient/PATIENT_ExternalBody.fbx`

- **Derived from:** `BaseHuman.blend`
- **Origin:** the embedded texture paths point at
  `.../Blender/2.76/scripts/addons/manuelbastionilab/data/shared_textures/`, which identifies
  the mesh as generated by **ManuelBastioniLab** (the predecessor of MB-Lab), Blender 2.76 era.
- **Licence:** ⚠️ **VERIFICAR.** Characters generated by the original ManuelBastioniLab were
  distributed under CC BY 4.0, but no licence file accompanied this download and the author is
  unknown. Confirm with whoever supplied the file before publishing.
- **Modifications:** SUBSURF and DISPLACE modifiers removed; `Teeth` and `Fur` material slots
  deleted; arms posed alongside the body; Armature and CorrectiveSmooth baked into a static
  mesh; re-oriented supine; single solid skin material applied. 28,964 triangles.

## Spine — `Assets/Models/Patient/PATIENT_Vertebrae.fbx`

- **Derived from:** `vertebrae.obj` (objects `Cervical_vertebra_(C4)`, `Thoracic_vertebra_(T7)`,
  `Lumbar_vertebra_(L3)`) from the **Z-Anatomy** project.
- **Licence:** **CC BY-SA 4.0** — <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>
- **Attribution:** "Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy — CC BY-SA 4.0".
  Z-Anatomy itself incorporates models derived from **BodyParts3D**, © The Database Center for
  Life Science, licensed **CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan**.
  Authors credited by Z-Anatomy: Kousaku Okubo (original BodyParts3D model),
  Gauthier Kervyn (design, 3D, anatomy), Marcin Zielinski (Blender add-on).
- **Modifications:** three vertebrae joined into one mesh, transformed into the patient's supine
  frame, single bone-coloured material applied.
- ⚠️ **ShareAlike obligation:** CC BY-SA is viral. Distributing a derivative of this mesh
  requires releasing that derivative under the same licence. This has direct consequences for
  how the project as a whole can be licensed — decide deliberately.
- Note: the Z-Anatomy distribution also bundles **non-commercial** components (a kidney under
  CC BY-NC 4.0 and an inner ear under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). **Neither is used here** — only the
  three vertebrae above were extracted.
